Modifications in this project:
Weather glyphs are selected from the official font, rasterized and resized
to 30 x 30 one-bit masks for the JD79668 four-color e-paper display.
Foreground colors and a black outline for yellow glyphs are applied locally.
The embedded browser catalogue uses the same masks as the firmware.

The original application in reference/jcalendar-main is not copied.
